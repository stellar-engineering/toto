Your agents keep working at home.

      
Toto is a small box on your own network that runs your coding agents around the clock. Shut the laptop. They carry on, and ask your phone when they need you.

      

        

        Counting Totos…
      

      
That number is live. Every Toto that is switched on holds a line open to the relay, and this page asks the relay how many there are.

    

    
  


  

    
What it is for

    

      

        
Work that outlasts your laptop

        
An agent halfway through a refactor does not care that you have gone to bed. It runs on the Toto, in your house, until it is done.

      

      

        
A yes or no from your pocket

        
When an agent wants to run something risky it stops and your phone buzzes. Allow it or deny it, and it carries on.

      

      

        
Yours, all the way down

        
Your hardware, your model account, your code. Nothing Toto says leaves your house readable by anyone but your phone.

      

    

  


  

    
The three parts

    

      

        
The box

        
A Raspberry Pi 4 or 5, or any small Linux machine. It keeps your projects, each walled off from the others, and runs the agents. Claude Code today; anything else by hand in a terminal you can open from your phone.

      

      

        
The app

        
Finds a new Toto over Bluetooth and puts it on your Wi‑Fi. After that it is where you start agents, read what they are doing, and say yes or no.

      

      

        
The relay

        
For when you are out. Your Toto and your phone both connect to it and it passes sealed messages between them. It cannot read them. You can run your own.
